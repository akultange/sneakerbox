# Sneakerbox — light wall

Spec demo for [Sneakerbox](https://www.facebook.com/sneakerboxonlineshop). Live at **https://akultange.github.io/sneakerbox/**

The shop's own on-foot photos form a dark wall. Light follows the finger (or cursor) and shows the colour; tap a pair for its price, the sizes in stock and an order that opens in their Messenger with the text already written. Pick a size and only the pairs in stock in that size light up. Georgian first, with an English toggle.

The intro is the shop's own box (the red creased paper and cream wordmark from their logo), with the headline printed on it: Georgian on the front, English on the back, and the logo big on the lid. It's built like a real one: board walls inside with the red paper turned in over the rim, the cut edge of the board along the rim, the lid's underside and inner rim, and two sheets of tissue paper folded in from the ends and sealed with a sticker.

On every open the box drops onto a lit stage and turns, floating, while the photos load. Each face darkens as it turns away from the key light, and you catch its end label as it turns (Sneakerbox, Tbilisi, EU sizes, pair count). Under it a barcode prints one bar per photo, with a rolling count. When the code is complete the box waits a moment to be opened ("Tap to open"): the tap is what lets it make a sound; left alone, it opens by itself, quietly. A scanner line reads the code, the box slows to face you, light leaks out under the lid, and the lid sticks for a moment, then lifts on tissue paper glowing from underneath. The box drops to the bottom of the screen, kicking up a little dust; the lid pops off, the tissue parts and a column of light stands up out of the box. The pairs come up out of it one by one and fly to their places on the wall, nearest first, each landing lit and settling into the dark, and the empty box sinks out of view. Once it's opening, a tap or a key skips to the wall; shared pair links go straight to the pair. The hint sits at the bottom of the wall and fades as you scroll.

Sound: every sound is made in the page with Web Audio, no files. Paper, board and air for the box (the scanner's beep, the lid, the landing, the tissue, a flick for each pair that flies out), a soft wooden note per tile as the light crosses the wall (a pentatonic scale, so moving across it plays), a shimmer for a filter's wave, the window opening and closing, a click for a size, a small bell when an order is ready, and a quiet room under it all that opens up a little as you move the light. It starts with the first tap or click (browsers allow nothing before); the switch in the header turns it off, and that's remembered.

With a mouse, the tile under the pointer lifts a little off the wall and settles back on the same spring when the pointer moves on. Picking a size or brand sends the light across the wall as a wave from the chip, and the name and price label glides from tile to tile.

Tap a pair and it opens in a window over the wall (a bottom sheet on phones). The photo flies in from its tile on a slight arc. The window has a lens on the photo with a mouse, the sizes in stock, a share button (the share sheet on phones, the link copied on a computer), the order button that lights up once a size is picked, and three more pairs to look at.

Prices are estimates from Tbilisi resellers and the sizes are placeholders until the shop sends its stock list.

## How it's built

- One WebGL2 canvas: the tile grid is drawn in a single fragment shader from a texture array, so the wall is one draw call at any size. A plain photo grid takes over where WebGL isn't available.
- The box is CSS 3D (no extra library); its loader spin, and the light turning on its faces, run on the compositor, so it stays smooth while the photos decode. Every intro animation is one per element and property, so none of it falls back to the main thread. The inside of the box isn't drawn until it starts to open. The tissue paper is a texture generated by `tools/make_box_textures.py` (crumpled folds, lit from underneath).
- No framework. `src/app.js` is the whole app, `src/style.css` and `src/index.html` the page.
- First screen ~130 KB (page, script, fonts, low-res wall); every tile sharp at ~630 KB. Sharp tiles load after the text has painted.

```
src/            index.html (template), style.css, app.js
assets/
  fonts/        Noto Sans Georgian (subset to Mkhedruli), Inter Tight, ₾ glyph
  img/p/        pair photos, 480/720/1080, AVIF + WebP
  img/t/        wall tiles, 160/320 WebP
  img/brand/    wordmark and icons cut from the shop's logo, the box's paper and tissue
  data/         catalog.json (generated)
tools/
  pairs.json         the stock list: pairs, prices, sizes, sold-out sizes, photo numbers
  process_images.py  grades the photos and writes assets/ + catalog.json
  build.py           writes dist/
  og.js              renders the 1200×630 share image from the live wall
  make_box_textures.py  the tissue paper inside the box
  qa.js, qa-size.js, qa-intro.js  headless checks (touch, history, fallback, size filter, the intro and its tap to open, the sound switch, the hint on scroll, window details)
  qa-sound.js        renders every sound offline and measures it (level, length, clipping)
  intro-frames.js    records the intro frame by frame
  scroll-frames.js   records a phone scroll (finger drags) frame by frame
  window-frames.js   captures the pair window opening, then a related pair
  hover-frames.js    records the mouse lift: onto a tile, across, and off
  shoot.js           phone + desktop screenshots
```

## Add or change a pair

1. Name the photos `pNNN_anything.jpg` (the 1080 px squares from Facebook are fine) and put them in one folder.
2. Add the pair to `tools/pairs.json`: `id`, `brand`, `model`, `color` (`ka` and `en`), `price`, `sizes`, `soldOut`, and `photos` (the NNN numbers).
3. `python3 tools/process_images.py <photo folder>`
4. `python3 tools/build.py https://akultange.github.io/sneakerbox/`
5. Publish `dist/` to the `gh-pages` branch.

## Work on it locally

```
npm install
python3 tools/build.py
python3 -m http.server 8765 --directory dist
node tools/qa.js http://127.0.0.1:8765/ qa
node tools/qa-size.js http://127.0.0.1:8765/ qa
node tools/qa-intro.js http://127.0.0.1:8765/ qa
node tools/qa-sound.js http://127.0.0.1:8765/ sounds.wav
```

The checks need Chromium: `@sparticuz/chromium` provides it.
